## Long description (≈120 words)
Habon is a calm habit tracker and daily routine planner for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Today is one glance and one tap. Track Yes/No, Count and Duration goals, set Limit goals for habits you want to cut back, and keep streaks that only count the days a habit is due. Group habits into morning, afternoon and evening routines, get reminders you can act on from the notification, and see week, month and year analytics. Your data stays on your device and there is no account. Free includes 2 habits and 1 routine with every goal type; Premium is optional. The Habon website also offers free habit tools that run in your browser: an online tracker, printable PDF trackers, a 30-day challenge and a routine planner.
