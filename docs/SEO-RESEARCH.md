# SEO and competitor research (US focus)

**Date:** 25 Nov 2026. **Method:** Google autocomplete (`suggestqueries`, `gl=us`, five prefix variants per seed), web searches for the target keywords, and reading result pages. 
**Not available to me:** search volumes, keyword difficulty, rankings. None are stated here. Autocomplete shows what people *type*, not how often.

## What the results look like
- **"habit tracker"** returns mostly App Store listings (Habit Tracker Grid, Habit Tracker by Davetech, Habitify, Streaks) and "best apps" roundups (Zapier, The Sweet Setup, Clockify, ClickUp). Branded apps and big content sites dominate.
- **Printable / template / PDF queries** are served by Clockify (20 templates with Google Sheets + PDF), ClickUp (18 templates; Sheets, Excel, Notion lists), Nir and Far (Google Sheets), plus many thin or mirrored "download" pages on unrelated domains. Quality is uneven, which is an opening for a clean, genuinely useful page.
- **"free online habit tracker no sign up"** returns small indie tools (Habitflow, Britetodo, abacktools, TrackTheHabit, iplan.to). Most are single-purpose pages with little supporting content.
- **"30 day habit challenge"** is led by long idea lists (Develop Good Habits 169 ideas, Psychology Today, Money Bliss, Clever Girl Finance). Few offer an interactive tracker or a printable sheet next to the ideas.
- **Daily routine planner free printable** is dominated by template-mirror domains; real planners with routines and times are scarce.

## Where Habon can realistically compete
1. **"free habit tracker app with unlimited habits (iPhone)"** is a real, recurring autocomplete theme. Habon's free tier is 2 habits, so we do **not** target this honestly as "unlimited". We target the adjacent intent ("free habit tracker iPhone") and state the limit clearly on `/free-habit-tracker/`. Trust beats a bait-and-switch.
2. **Printable long-tail** (monthly, A4, A5, with graph, 2026, editable). We cover weekly/monthly, A4 and Letter. **Gaps to add:** A5 size, a yearly (annual) tracker, an "editable" explanation, and a printable with graph.
3. **Templates for Google Sheets / Excel / Notion / Apple Numbers.** Heavily searched, content-led competitors. **Gap:** we have PDFs only. Next best step: publish a free CSV/XLSX tracker template download and a "Habit tracker template for Notion/Sheets" guide.
4. **Interactive 30-day challenge + ideas + printable sheet** in one place is rare. Done.
5. **Apple-specific long tail:** "habit tracker apple watch", "habit tracker app ios", "habit tracker with widget". Habon has an Apple Watch app (can claim). We have **not** claimed widgets because the listing does not mention them.
6. **ADHD-oriented queries** ("habit tracker app adhd") appear often. Only worth targeting with honest, evidence-aware content, not medical claims.
7. **Routine checklists** (evening, bedtime, kids) are broad. Our generators and guides cover adults; a kids' routine chart page is a later opportunity.

## Cannibalization controls used
- One primary query per page (see table below). Overlapping intents were merged into distinct jobs: `/habit-tracker/` = guide hub, `/online-habit-tracker/` = full tool, `/daily-habit-tracker/` = today-only tool view, `/free-habit-tracker/` = comparison of free options, `/printable-habit-tracker/` = generator, `/habit-tracker-pdf/` = one-click downloads, `/habit-tracker-templates/` = which-template guide.
- All tool pages have unique copy, FAQs and distinct functionality.
