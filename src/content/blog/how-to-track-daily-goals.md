Daily goals go wrong in two ways: too many, or too vague. A short, clear list and a quick review habit fix both.

## Step 1: Limit yourself to three

Pick three goals for the day. One can be a must-do for work or study, one a habit like a walk, and one something for you. More than three and most of the list turns into hope.

## Step 2: Make each one checkable

"Work on the report" is vague. "Write the first two pages of the report" is checkable. Ask: how will I know it is done?

## Step 3: Separate tasks from habits

- A one-off item goes on a short to-do list. See [habit tracker vs to-do list](/blog/habit-tracker-vs-to-do-list/).
- A repeating behavior goes in a [habit tracker](/online-habit-tracker/).

Mixing them clutters both.

## Step 4: Connect daily goals to bigger ones

If a daily action does not point at anything, it will not last. Use the [goal planner](/goal-planner/) to keep the outcome and its milestones in view, and choose two or three weekly actions that move it forward. Track those actions as habits.

## Step 5: Write tomorrow's list tonight

Spend five minutes at the end of the day writing tomorrow's three goals. You wake up with a plan, and an unfinished item from today is easy to carry over deliberately instead of by accident.

## Step 6: Review each evening

Two questions:

1. What did I finish?
2. What stopped me from finishing the rest?

Do not argue with yourself. Just note the cause, because it shows what to change.

## Step 7: Review each week

Look at seven days together. Patterns appear: Tuesday is always overloaded, mornings work better than evenings, one goal keeps carrying over. Adjust the system, not your self-judgment. See the [weekly habit planner](/weekly-habit-planner/).

## Choosing how to track

- **Paper:** a daily page with three lines.
- **Web:** the [daily habit tracker](/daily-habit-tracker/) for habits.
- **Phone:** an app with reminders and the right goal types. Habon supports Yes/No, Count and Duration goals, plus Limit goals. See [features](/features/).

## Avoiding burnout

- Keep one rest day a week with a lighter list.
- Use the smallest version on bad days.
- Celebrate finishing, not just perfect days.
