A 30-day challenge is a short sprint on one habit. It works because it has a clear start, a clear finish and a visible chain of days. Here is how to set one up so you finish it.

## Step 1: Choose one habit

Pick one, not three. Good challenge habits are small and daily: drink eight glasses of water, walk 20 minutes, read 10 pages, stretch for five minutes, journal three lines. Browse the ready-made list in the [30-day habit challenge tool](/30-day-habit-challenge/), or the [habit ideas generator](/habit-ideas-generator/).

## Step 2: Define done

Write what counts: *"A day counts if I walk for 20 minutes in total."* A clear rule removes daily negotiation.

## Step 3: Set a near start date

Start within a few days. Waiting for the perfect Monday or the first of the month is a classic delay. You can start on a Thursday.

## Step 4: Attach it to a cue

Anchor the habit to something you already do. *After I brush my teeth, I stretch for five minutes.*

## Step 5: Make the bad-day version

Decide the smallest possible version you will do when you have no time or energy. For a 20-minute walk, it might be five minutes around the block. This is what protects the chain.

## Step 6: Make it visible

Print the sheet and put it where you will see it, or track it in the tool. The [printable challenge sheet](/habit-tracker-pdf/) has 30 numbered circles to fill in.

## Step 7: Check in daily, review weekly

Tick the day at a fixed time. Every seven days, ask what is working and what is in the way, and adjust.

## What to do if you miss a day

Carry on the next day. One miss does not end a challenge. Two in a row is the thing to avoid. You can mark the missed circle and continue, and finishing 27 of 30 is still a meaningful result.

## What the research says about "30 days"

The idea that habits take 21 days is a myth. A University College London study of 96 people found it took between 18 and 254 days for a new behavior to feel automatic, with a median of 66 days, and the time varied by person and habit. A 30-day challenge is a useful first step, not a guarantee, and it is fine to continue past day 30.

## Day 30: decide what is next

Pick one:

1. **Keep it** as an ongoing daily habit.
2. **Raise the bar slightly**, for example from 5 to 10 minutes.
3. **Retire it** if it did its job.
4. **Start a new challenge**, one at a time.
