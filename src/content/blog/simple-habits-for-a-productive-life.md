Productivity is mostly boring consistency. These ten habits are small, take under ten minutes each and support the rest of your work and life. Pick one or two to start.

## 1. Write tomorrow's top three tasks (5 minutes)

At the end of the day, write the three things that matter most tomorrow. You wake up with direction instead of a blank slate.

## 2. Do the hardest task first

Before email, before messages, do the task you are most likely to avoid. Even 25 minutes of focused work early makes the day feel productive.

## 3. Use focus blocks (25 minutes)

Set a timer for 25 minutes and work on one thing, then take a short break. The structure makes it easier to begin.

## 4. Review your calendar each morning (3 minutes)

Know what is fixed, where the gaps are and where to put focus time.

## 5. Process your inbox once or twice a day

Batch email and messages instead of reacting all day. Turn off nonessential notifications.

## 6. Take a walk (10 minutes)

A short walk is an easy way to move, think and reset between tasks.

## 7. Tidy your workspace at the end of the day (5 minutes)

Starting tomorrow with a clear desk removes friction.

## 8. Keep a consistent sleep time

A steady bedtime and wake time supports energy and focus. Adults generally need at least seven hours of sleep. See [how to build an evening routine](/blog/how-to-build-an-evening-routine/).

## 9. Do a weekly review (15 minutes)

Once a week, look at what you finished, what slipped and what is next. Adjust your plan. This one habit makes the other nine work better.

## 10. Write down one win (2 minutes)

At the end of the day, note something that went well. It keeps you from only seeing what is left undone.

## How to adopt them without overload

- Choose **one** to begin with.
- Anchor it to an existing event.
- Track it for a month in the [online habit tracker](/online-habit-tracker/).
- Add a second only when the first feels routine.
