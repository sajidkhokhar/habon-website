The best daily habit is small, specific and something you actually want. Here are 50 ideas grouped by area. Pick one, not ten. Then write down when and where you will do it.

Want a random pick? Use the [habit ideas generator](/habit-ideas-generator/) and add any idea to your tracker with one click.

## Health

1. Drink a glass of water after waking.
2. Eat a piece of fruit with lunch.
3. Take vitamins with breakfast.
4. Floss before bed.
5. Eat one extra serving of vegetables.
6. Keep a water bottle on your desk and refill it twice.

## Fitness

7. Walk for 20 minutes.
8. Do 10 push-ups.
9. Take the stairs.
10. Stretch for 5 minutes.
11. Do 10 minutes of yoga.
12. Hit a daily step goal you chose.

## Mind

13. Meditate for 5 minutes.
14. Write three lines in a journal.
15. Write down one thing you are grateful for.
16. Take five slow breaths before checking your phone.
17. Spend 10 minutes outside.
18. Name one win from the day.

## Learning

19. Read 10 pages.
20. Practice a language for 10 minutes.
21. Practice an instrument for 15 minutes.
22. Learn one new word.
23. Review flashcards for five minutes.
24. Write down one thing you learned today.

## Productivity

25. Write your top three tasks for tomorrow.
26. Do your hardest task first.
27. Work one 25-minute focus block.
28. Clear your inbox once a day.
29. Review your calendar each morning.
30. Tidy your desk when you finish work.

## Home

31. Make the bed.
32. Wash the dishes after dinner.
33. Tidy for 10 minutes.
34. Wipe the kitchen counter.
35. Lay out tomorrow's clothes.
36. Declutter one item.

## Social

37. Message a friend or family member.
38. Give one genuine compliment.
39. Put your phone away at dinner.
40. Say thank you to someone.

## Money

41. Log your spending.
42. Wait 24 hours before buying anything non-essential.
43. Check your account balance.
44. Pack lunch.

## Sleep

45. Go to bed at the same time.
46. Put your phone away 30 minutes before bed.
47. Get morning daylight.
48. Skip caffeine after 2 pm.
49. Dim the lights an hour before sleep.
50. Write tomorrow's to-do list before bed.

## How to choose one

Ask three questions:

- **Can I do it in under five minutes on a bad day?** If not, shrink it.
- **Do I have a natural cue?** After coffee, after brushing teeth, when I sit down at my desk.
- **Do I care about it?** A habit you chose beats a habit you felt you should have.

Then follow [how to build better habits](/blog/how-to-build-better-habits/) and track it for 30 days with the [30-day habit challenge](/30-day-habit-challenge/).
