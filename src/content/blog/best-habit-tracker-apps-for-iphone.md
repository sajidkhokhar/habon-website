There is no single best habit tracker for iPhone, because the best one is the one you will open tomorrow. Instead of a ranked list, this guide gives you the criteria that matter, then a short list of well-known options to try. We make Habon, so we say so up front, and we only describe other apps using what their own listings and independent reviewers report.

## What matters in a habit tracker app

**1. Speed of the daily check-in.** If logging takes more than a few seconds, you will stop. Look for a screen where today's habits are one tap each.

**2. The goal types it supports.** Plenty of habits are not yes/no. You may want a count (8 glasses of water), a duration (30 minutes of reading) or a limit (no more than two coffees). Check that the app handles the kinds of habits you have.

**3. How it counts streaks.** Some apps reset a streak on any missed day. Others only count the days a habit is due, so a Monday/Wednesday/Friday habit is not "broken" on Tuesday. Pick the model that matches how you live.

**4. Reminders you can act on.** A notification that lets you mark a habit done without opening the app saves a step.

**5. Apple Watch and Health.** If you want to check habits from your wrist or see steps and workouts beside your habits, confirm the app does that.

**6. Free limits and pricing.** Many habit apps are free to download but cap the number of habits or lock features behind a subscription. Before you build a routine, find out where the limit is. Check the current price in the App Store, because prices change.

**7. Privacy.** Look for what data stays on your device, whether you need an account and what the App Store privacy label says.

## How to test an app in a week

1. Add only two habits.
2. Check in at the same time every day.
3. Note whether you needed to think about the app, or whether it was one tap.
4. At the end of the week, ask whether you would miss it.
