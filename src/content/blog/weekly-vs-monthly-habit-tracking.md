Weekly and monthly habit trackers use the same idea at different scales. Neither is better in every case. The right choice depends on how many habits you have, how often you review and what you want to see.

## Weekly tracking

**What it shows:** seven days at a glance, with larger boxes and room for notes.

**Strengths**

- Short feedback loop: you can fix a bad week next week.
- Big, easy-to-read boxes.
- Natural weekly review on Sunday.
- Good for beginners and for two to six habits.

**Limits**

- Less sense of the long run.
- You need a new sheet every week if you print.

## Monthly tracking

**What it shows:** every day of the month in one grid.

**Strengths**

- Reveals long-run patterns, such as dips on certain weekdays or around busy weeks.
- Keeps a visible chain for streak-minded people.
- One page covers the month.

**Limits**

- Smaller boxes.
- A bad start to the month can feel discouraging, so people give up.
- Reviews can feel distant if you only look at the end of the month.

## Quick comparison

| | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Review rhythm | Every Sunday | End of the month |
| Best for | Beginners, few habits | Spotting patterns, many habits |
| Room for notes | Plenty | Little |
| Risk | Missing the big picture | Losing motivation after a bad start |

## How to combine them

Many people use both: a **monthly** sheet for the big picture and the long chain, and a **weekly** sheet or planner for the review and planning. Try the [weekly habit planner](/weekly-habit-planner/) and [monthly habit calendar](/monthly-habit-calendar/), both free in the browser, and print either as a PDF.

## Weekly and monthly targets within a habit

Weekly and monthly can also describe the habit itself. "Exercise three times a week" is a weekly-target habit. "Deep-clean the kitchen once a month" is a monthly-target habit. The [online habit tracker](/online-habit-tracker/) counts streaks for weeks or months that hit the target.

## Which should you choose?

- **Starting out:** weekly.
- **Many habits or long streaks:** monthly.
- **You want both accountability and a bigger picture:** weekly sheet plus a monthly review.
