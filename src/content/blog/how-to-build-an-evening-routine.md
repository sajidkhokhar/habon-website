An evening routine does two jobs: it winds the day down and it sets up tomorrow. Done well, it makes falling asleep easier and mornings less rushed.

## Step 1: Pick a target bedtime

Work backward from when you need to wake up. Major US sleep organizations recommend that adults get at least seven hours of sleep a night, so choose a bedtime that allows that, then build your routine to finish at that time. The [evening routine generator](/evening-routine-generator/) schedules steps backward from your bedtime so the last step ends when you want to be in bed.

## Step 2: Close the day

Spend five minutes getting tomorrow out of your head:

- Write the top three tasks for tomorrow.
- Note one thing that went well.
- Jot down anything you are worried about so it is on paper, not in your head.

## Step 3: Prepare tomorrow

Every decision you make tonight is one you will not make while half-awake tomorrow:

- Lay out clothes.
- Pack your bag.
- Fill your water bottle.
- Set out breakfast items.

This is the quiet link to a good [morning routine](/blog/how-to-build-a-morning-routine/).

## Step 4: Wind down

Dim the lights, put the phone down and do something calm. Reading, stretching, a warm shower or a few minutes of breathing all work. The aim is a clear signal to your body that the day is over. Keep the phone charging away from the bed if you can.

## Step 5: Keep it short and repeatable

A 30 to 45 minute routine is plenty. If it takes longer, people skip it. Remember that a smaller routine you do nightly beats an elaborate one you do twice a week.

## A sample 45-minute routine

| Time before bed | Step |
|---|---|
| 45 min | Tidy the main living space for 10 minutes |
| 35 min | Prepare clothes and bag |
| 30 min | Write tomorrow's top three tasks |
| 25 min | Screens off |
| 20 min | Warm shower |
| 10 min | Skincare and teeth |
| 5 min | Read a few pages |
| 0 | Lights out |

## Common problems

- **"I get absorbed in my phone."** Make the first step physical: charge it in another room, then do the next step.
- **"I am not tired at bedtime."** Keep the wake time steady, get morning daylight and avoid caffeine late in the day. If sleep trouble persists, talk to a doctor.
- **"The routine feels like homework."** Cut it to three steps.
